A freshly built directory almost always shows zero visitors for a while. That is normal, and most of the wait is on Google's side — but only if two things are true first. This guide covers what to do, in order, and what to expect.

## 1. Connect your own domain

Directories served from a Directify subdomain (for example `yourdirectory.directify.xyz`) are **hidden from search engines**: every page carries a `noindex` tag. That keeps half-built directories and duplicates out of Google's index, but it also means nothing you publish there can rank until you connect a domain.

Follow [Connect a Custom Domain](/directories/custom-domain). Once the domain is verified, the `noindex` tag is removed automatically and your subdomain redirects to the domain.

<Info>
  Custom domains are included on every paid plan. If you don't own a domain yet, a `.com` costs about $10/year at Cloudflare, Namecheap, or Porkbun.
</Info>

## 2. Tell Google about it

Google will find a new domain on its own eventually, but you can shorten the wait considerably:

1. Open [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) and add your domain as a property (the **Domain** property type, verified with the DNS record Google gives you).
2. Go to **Sitemaps** and submit `https://yourdomain.com/sitemap.xml`. Directify generates and updates the sitemap for you.
3. Optional: paste your homepage URL into **URL inspection** and click **Request indexing**.

**What to expect:** Google usually starts sending the first organic visitors **2–6 weeks** after it discovers the domain. Directories with more listings and more categories tend to get there faster because there are more pages to rank. Check **Performance** in Search Console — impressions show up before clicks do, and that is your sign it is working.

<Tip>
  The [SEO settings](/settings/routes) page in your dashboard shows your sitemap URL and lets you set per-page titles and descriptions. Filling in the homepage meta description is the single cheapest SEO win.
</Tip>

## 3. Share the link

The fastest first 100 visitors come from you, not from Google:

- **Tell the businesses you listed.** Email or DM each one a link to their listing. Most will share it, and a claimed listing is a listing that gets kept up to date. Enable [listing claims](/listings/add-listing) so they can take ownership.
- **Post where your niche already is.** A subreddit, a Facebook group, a Discord, a LinkedIn community, a local newsletter. Lead with the useful part ("here are all 80 dog groomers in Austin, with hours and reviews") rather than the product.
- **Answer questions with it.** Whenever someone asks "does anyone know a good…" in your niche, your directory is the answer.
- **Add it to your other profiles.** Your email signature, your social bios, your personal site.

## Where you are on the path

Your dashboard's **Analytics** page shows the visitors you have today. While the total is under 20 you will see a *Your first 100 visitors* card there with these three steps and their status.

If you have had a verified domain for more than six weeks and still see no impressions in Search Console, something else is wrong — reach out on [Discord](https://discord.gg/B9hTD4kAJY) or email support and we will look at it with you.
