## Adding Social Links

To add social links to your directory:

1. In your dashboard, go to **Manage → Directories** and open your directory
2. Scroll down to the **Social Media** section and click its heading to expand it (it is collapsed by default)
3. Click **Add to Social Links**
4. Select the platform from the dropdown
5. Enter your profile URL
6. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the page

<Note>
Always enter the full URL including `https://`, for example `https://x.com/directify`.
</Note>

Your social links will appear in your directory's footer and can help:
- Build your social media following
- Increase brand visibility
- Provide additional ways to contact you
- Share more content with your audience
