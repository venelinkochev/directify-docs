Google Maps Import lets you populate your directory with real businesses in minutes. Search for something like **"plumbers in Austin, TX"**, and Google returns up to 60 real businesses with their name, address, phone number, website, opening hours and map coordinates. Select the ones you want and import them as listings — then invite those businesses to claim their listing.

You bring your own Google API key and Google bills your account directly for what you use. Google's free monthly tier comfortably covers typical imports (roughly 15–20 searches-plus-imports per month at no cost).

<Note>
Google Maps Import is available on the **Pro plan and above**.
</Note>

## Step 1: Create a Google API key

This is the only technical part, and you only do it once. The key lives in your own Google account, so your searches and imports never share quota with anyone else.

1. Go to the [Google Cloud Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/) and sign in with any Google account
2. Create a project (top bar → project dropdown → **New project**) — name it anything, e.g. "My Directory"
3. Enable the Places API: open [Places API (New)](https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/library/places.googleapis.com) and click **Enable**. Make sure it's the one called **"Places API (New)"** — Google also lists an older "Places API" that won't work
4. Set up billing if the console asks for it (**Billing** in the left menu). A card is required by Google even for free-tier usage — you won't be charged within the free monthly allowance
5. Create the key: go to [Credentials](https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials) → **Create credentials** → **API key**. Copy the key (it starts with `AIza`)

<Tip>
**Recommended:** click **Edit API key** after creating it and, under *API restrictions*, restrict it to the **Places API (New)** only. If the key ever leaks, it can't be used for anything else.
</Tip>

## Step 2: Connect the key in Directify

1. In your directory panel, go to **Content → Google Maps Import**
2. Paste the API key and click **Save & validate key**

Directify makes a minimal test request to confirm the key works before saving it. The key is stored encrypted, and you can disconnect it at any time — listings you already imported are not affected.

If validation fails, the two usual causes are: the **Places API (New)** isn't enabled on the key's project (step 3 above), or **billing** isn't set up (step 4 above).

## Step 3: Search and import

1. Type a niche plus a location — e.g. `coffee shops in Portland, OR` or `zahnärzte in Berlin` (any language works)
2. Browse the results and tick the businesses you want. Use **Load more results** to fetch up to 60 in total, and **Select all** / **Clear selection** to work faster. Businesses you've already imported are marked and can't be imported twice
3. Choose your import options and click **Import selected listings**

### Import options

- **Assign to category** — attach every imported listing to one of your categories. You can refine per-listing categories afterwards
- **Publish immediately** — off by default: listings are imported as **drafts**, so you can review and edit them before they go live on your site
- **Also import Google reviews** — see below

The import runs in the background; you'll get a notification (and an email) when it finishes, with a summary of what was imported and skipped.

### What gets imported

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Name | The business name |
| Address & city | Full formatted address, plus the city for location features |
| Map coordinates | Latitude/longitude — listings appear on your map views right away |
| Phone number | As listed on Google Maps |
| Website | Becomes the listing's URL |
| Opening hours | Stored in your `hours_of_operation` custom field, if your directory has one (the Local Business template ships with it) |

Photos are **not** imported — Google Maps photos belong to the people who uploaded them. Set a [custom default listing image](/directories/customize-your-directory) or add your own images per listing.

## Importing Google reviews

Tick **"Also import Google reviews"** to copy up to 5 reviews per listing (that's the maximum Google provides). Reviews are imported already-approved with the author's name, a link to their Google profile, the star rating, the review text and the original review date — they show up on your listing pages immediately.

Re-importing a listing never duplicates its reviews, and you can delete imported reviews at any time from your Reviews page.

<Warning>
**Know the trade-off before importing reviews.** Google's Terms of Service don't permit storing review content permanently, and each review is its author's own text. Importing copies these reviews into your directory through your own Google API key — the choice, and the responsibility, are yours. Directify always stores and displays the author's name with a link to their Google profile.
</Warning>

## Costs

Your key is billed by Google, not by Directify. As a rule of thumb:

- **One search** (up to 60 results) costs about **$0.10** in Google's pricing
- **Importing with reviews** adds roughly **$0.04 per imported listing**

Google's free monthly tier covers roughly 15–20 typical search-and-import sessions per month at **$0**. You can monitor usage anytime in the Google Cloud Console under *APIs & Services*.

## Good to know

- **The data becomes yours to maintain.** Imported listings are regular listings — they don't stay in sync with Google, and under Google's terms, long-term storage of the imported details is your responsibility as the API key owner
- **Duplicates are handled automatically** — every imported listing remembers its Google place ID, so re-running the same search skips businesses you already have
- **Plan limits apply** — imports count toward your plan's listing limit; anything over the limit is skipped and reported in the completion notification
- **Great with claims** — import the businesses in your niche, then let them [claim their listing](/listings/add-listing) to take ownership and keep it updated
